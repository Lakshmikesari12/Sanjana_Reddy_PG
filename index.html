About Us

Welcome to Sanjana Reddy Luxury Women's PG & Hostel, a trusted accommodation designed exclusively for women seeking a safe, comfortable, and homely living environment in Gachibowli.

Our PG offers fully furnished rooms with multiple sharing options, modern amenities, nutritious home-style meals, high-speed Wi-Fi, regular housekeeping, laundry services, CCTV surveillance, and 24/7 security to ensure a worry-free stay.

Conveniently located near major IT companies, colleges, shopping centers, hospitals, and public transportation, our hostel is an ideal choice for working professionals and students.

We focus on providing a clean, peaceful, and friendly atmosphere where residents can feel at home while pursuing their education and career goals.

At Sanjana Reddy Women's PG, we believe in offering not just a room, but a comfortable lifestyle with safety, convenience, and quality living at affordable prices.
